What is a Shippuu Speedster?

This community was created with the theme of a dark pot of TAM's favorite things.
Currently, we are active in three main areas: Touhou Project, Asphalt9, and game production such as Unity.
Please join us in the future as we may have more genres to handle as we add more members!
We also send out other official TAMGameCreator information. Join us and check out the latest information!
